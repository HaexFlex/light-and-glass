HOW TO PLAY
Fill every pane of the dark window with the right colour of glass.
• Some panes start lit. A lit pane shows little coloured numbers: how many of its neighbouring panes have each colour.
• Neighbours are the panes that share a lead-line border with it.
• Place a pane correctly and it lights up, showing its own numbers. A wrong colour cracks and falls out.
• Tap a lit pane to highlight its neighbours.
• Everything can be worked out by logic – no guessing.
• Each colour also has a small symbol (on its gem and in the numbers), so similar shades are easy to tell apart.



Tap a colour, then tap panes. Pinch to zoom, drag with one finger to move, ⤢ toggles zoom.
