Light & Glass

a stained-glass logic puzzle
HOW TO PLAY
Fill every pane of the dark window with the right colour of glass.
• Pips beside a row / below a column: how many panes of each colour it holds (? = not told).
• A number on a lit pane: how many of its 4 neighbours (up, down, left, right) share its colour.
• Some panes start lit. A lit pane shows little coloured numbers: how many of its 8 surrounding panes (diagonals too)neighbouring panes – those sharing a lead-line border with it (touching only at a corner doesn’t count) have each colour.
• Place a pane correctly and it lights up, showing its own numbers. A wrong colour cracks and falls out.
• Everything can be worked out by logic – no guessing.

Tap a colour, then tap panes. Tap again or long-press to clear; drag to paint several. Pinch to zoom, drag with one finger to move when zoomed, ⤢ toggles zoom.
